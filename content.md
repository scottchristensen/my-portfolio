## About Scott

Scott Christensen is a growth design leader based in Salt Lake City with 15 years of experience connecting design craft to strategy and growth. He is cofounder of [SendSight](https://sendsight.ai), which automates lifecycle journey insights for growth teams.

Previously: Design Lead, Growth at [Mercury](https://mercury.com) (2023–2025); Head of Product Design at [H1](https://h1.co) (2020–2022); Founding Designer at [Mystery](https://www.trymystery.com) (2019–2020); Lead Designer / Design Manager at Egencia, an Expedia company (2018–2019); Product Designer / Sr. Designer at PwC Experience Center (2015–2018); Management Consultant at PwC's Strategy& (2013–2015). He helped start [GrowthDesigners.co](https://growthdesigners.co), a community for growth designers, by creating its Slack community in 2018, and has spent about eight years helping build it to 2,000+ members. He also co-leads [Growth Design School](https://www.growthdesigners.co/school), the community's growth design course.

Education: Brigham Young University (BYU), business strategy, graduated 2013. He moved from management consulting into design and is self-taught as a designer.

Profiles: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/scottcharleschristensen/) · [X @_ScottC_](https://x.com/_ScottC_) · [GitHub](https://github.com/scottchristensen) · [Medium @ScottC](https://medium.com/@ScottC)

Not to be confused with other people named Scott Christensen, including a painter of the same name. This Scott Christensen is the product and growth designer described on scottchristensen.design.

## Interviews and talks

### [Beyond UX Design: Broad Strokes Before Fine Lines](https://www.beyonduxdesign.com/episode/92-the-generalists-advantage-with-scott-christensen/)
August 2024 · podcast with Jeremy Miller · 58 min. Published as ep. 92, "The Generalist's Advantage". Recorded while Scott was lead growth designer at Mercury. He argues against niching down early in a UX career and proposes a career "double diamond": go broad first, then specialise. Topics include a competence framework, "there are no gatekeepers: gatekeep yourself", the skills a well-rounded UX designer needs, how to choose what to specialise in, and three heuristics for knowing whether you're a good growth designer.

July 2023 · livestream.

### [SaaStock Presentation with Jonathan Anderson, CEO @ Candu.ai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RiGEkgsUFdk)
June 2023 · Austin, TX · 14 min. Published as "PLG Activation Best Practices". Scott and Jonathan Anderson tear down Airtable's onboarding flow to illustrate product-led-growth activation practices.

### [Growth Design at Startups, with Emmanuel Omole](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/04-growth-designer-at-early-stage-startup-scott-christensen/id1659270905?i=1000605939008)
March 2023 · podcast with Emmanuel Omole · 37 min. Published as Grow the Product, ep. 4, "Growth Designer at Early-Stage Startup". Recorded while Scott ran his own growth design consultancy. Covers the stages of a startup and what each means for growth, what a growth designer should look for when deciding which startup to join, how to hone your craft at an early-stage company, and go-to-market considerations.

### [Is Early-Stage Growth Design for You? with Ran Liu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lz6W4Fq3Msk)
March 2023 · live broadcast · 59 min. GrowthDesigners.co community event, published as "Is Early-Stage Startup Meant for You?". A live discussion following Scott's March 2023 GrowthDesigners newsletter: how growth differs at early vs. later-stage companies, whether you'll hone your craft as a growth designer at an early-stage company, and what to look for when choosing a startup.

## Community: GrowthDesigners.co

[GrowthDesigners.co](https://growthdesigners.co) is a community for designers focused on blending growth strategy and design to make a lasting business impact. Scott helped start it by creating the Slack community in 2018, and has helped build it to 2,000+ members over about eight years. It offers a Slack community for asynchronous discussion, mentorship, and free events, a monthly newsletter on growth design topics, and Growth Design School.

[Growth Design School](https://www.growthdesigners.co/school) launched in 2023. It's a remote growth design masterclass: about 5 hours of video, case studies, assignments, and live debriefs, covering foundations, the scientific design process, acquisition, activation and engagement, conversion, and ethical design. Scott co-leads and teaches it with Chase Denomme and Molly Norris Walker.
